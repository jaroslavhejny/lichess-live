# Chess Broadcast Widget Support

Chess Broadcast Widget helps you follow Lichess broadcasts on your Mac.

## Contact

For help, feedback, or privacy questions, contact **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

Please do not include your Lichess access token in support requests.

## Getting started

1. Open the app and choose a broadcast and a game.
2. Add **Chess Position & Game** from the macOS widget gallery.
3. Enable notifications in the app if you want game updates, and allow them in macOS when prompted.

Keep the app window open and your Mac awake for live monitoring and notifications. macOS controls widget refreshes, so the displayed position may be delayed.
