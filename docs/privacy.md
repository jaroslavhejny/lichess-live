Last updated: [DATE]

## Lichess requests

The app connects to Lichess to retrieve broadcasts, game positions, and related information. If you use followed-player features, the app also requests your following list and player statuses. These requests are made to Lichess and are subject to its [privacy policy](https://lichess.org/privacy). Like other internet services, Lichess may receive network information such as your IP address.

## Optional access token

You may add a Lichess personal access token in Settings. The app stores the token in macOS Keychain and sends it to Lichess when making authenticated requests, including when checking the token. You can remove the saved token in Settings and revoke it through your Lichess account. The widget may make anonymous requests to Lichess when fetching a stale position independently.

## Information on your Mac

The app stores settings, your selected game, and game position information locally. The app and its widget share the information needed to display the selected game through a macOS App Group. Stockfish position analysis runs locally on your Mac.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications and grant macOS permission, the app can display notifications about games and followed-player status. You can turn these off in the app or in macOS System Settings.
